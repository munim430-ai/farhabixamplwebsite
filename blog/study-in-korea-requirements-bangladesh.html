Study in Korea: Requirements for Bangladeshi Students (2026)

Guide 18 Aug 2026 Hangeul
Academic qualifications, language, documents, and costs — everything a Bangladeshi student needs to qualify to study in South Korea, explained simply.

Thinking of studying in South Korea? This guide lays out exactly what a Bangladeshi student needs — academically, financially, and in documents — to get admitted and reach a Korean campus in 2026.

1. Academic requirements

  • Korean Language Program (KLP): completion of HSC / equivalent is generally enough to start.
  • Bachelor's degree: HSC / A-Levels / Diploma with a strong result.
  • Master's degree: a completed Bachelor's degree.
  • PhD: a completed Master's degree.

A good GPA matters — many universities and scholarships have minimum grade cut-offs.

2. Language requirements

  • Korean-taught programs: usually TOPIK Level 3–4+. The KLP exists to get you there.
  • English-taught programs: IELTS/TOEFL scores are often accepted instead of Korean.

Even for English-taught degrees, basic Korean makes daily life far easier — and strengthens your visa case.

3. Financial requirements

You'll need to show you can fund your studies and living costs — typically bank statements equivalent to around USD 10,000–20,000, or a scholarship like GKS. This "financial proof" is also central to your student visa.

4. Documents you'll prepare

  • Passport (6+ months validity) and photos
  • Academic certificates and transcripts (often attested/notarized)
  • Language scores (TOPIK / IELTS / TOEFL)
  • Statement of purpose / study plan
  • Bank statements or scholarship documents
  • Certificate of Admission (after you're accepted)

5. The step-by-step process

  1. Choose your program and universities
  2. Learn Korean and take TOPIK (or prepare IELTS for English-taught)
  3. Apply and receive your Certificate of Admission
  4. Apply for your student visa (D-2 / D-4) at the Korean Embassy in Dhaka
  5. Fly to Korea and begin your studies
