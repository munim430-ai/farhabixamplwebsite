Why South Korea

Why Thousands Choose South Korea

World-class education, affordable costs, government scholarships, and strong career pathways for international students.

🏫

World-Class Universities

Seoul National University, KAIST, Yonsei, and Korea University rank in the global top 200. Korean degrees are internationally recognized and respected by employers worldwide.

💰

Scholarships & Affordability

The GKS scholarship covers full tuition, living allowance, and language training. Even without scholarships, Korea is more affordable than Western study destinations.

💼

Career & PR Opportunities

Korea’s booming tech and manufacturing economy offers strong post-study work visas. Many graduates transition to employment at Samsung, LG, Hyundai, and leading Korean firms.

The ideal first step for students with no prior Korean knowledge. We start from Basic Korean (Hangul reading & writing), progress through conversational levels, and prepare students for TOPIK Level 1 & 2. Requirements

Admission Requirements

Eligibility criteria for each program. Read carefully before applying.

Academic Qualification
  • Must complete HSC / Diploma (4 years) to apply
  • Alim Certificate accepted only if from science background
  • Passing year 2023 or later — GPA must be above 3.5
  • If SSC GPA below 3.5, HSC GPA must be above 4.0
  • Vocational Certificate not accepted
  • China diploma accepted if equivalent to or higher than 12th grade
  • Date of Birth must be 2004 (2003 accepted with TOPIK certificate)
Financial Qualification
  • Only parents can be the sponsor
  • Bank balance must be in parent's or student's name
  • Savings account required
  • Multiple accounts allowed — minimum 5 lac BDT in each
  • FDR can be submitted alongside savings account
  • Account must show minimum 18 lac BDT as last balance
Other Qualification
  • Name mismatch between parents and academic certificate — affidavit accepted. Completely different name must be corrected before applying
  • Family relationship certificate must be under student's name with parents' names matching NID/Passport
