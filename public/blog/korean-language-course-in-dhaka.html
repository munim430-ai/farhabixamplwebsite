Korean Language Course in Dhaka: Fees, Levels & TOPIK (2026 Guide)

Guide 18 Aug 2026 Hangeul
Fees, TOPIK levels, and how to choose the right Korean language course in Dhaka — plus how language study connects to studying in Korea.

Learning Korean is the first real step toward studying, working, or building a future in South Korea. If you are searching for a Korean language course in Dhaka, this guide explains the levels, the fees, the TOPIK exam, and how to choose the right course for your goal.

Why learn Korean in Bangladesh?

Korean opens doors that few other languages do for Bangladeshi students. It is required for the Korean Language Program (KLP) at most universities, it strengthens your student-visa application, and it is essential for the EPS-TOPIK employment route. Even for English-taught degrees, basic Korean makes daily life in Korea far easier.

Korean language levels (TOPIK)

Korean proficiency is measured by TOPIK (Test of Proficiency in Korean), from Level 1 (beginner) to Level 6 (advanced):

  • TOPIK I (Levels 1–2): Beginner — greetings, basic grammar, everyday conversation.
  • TOPIK II (Levels 3–6): Intermediate to advanced — used for university admission and jobs.

Most universities ask for at least TOPIK Level 3–4 for degree programs, while the KLP starts you from the beginning.

How much does a Korean language course cost in Dhaka?

Fees in Dhaka generally range from BDT 4,000 to BDT 35,000 depending on the level, intensity, and institution. Beginner (Sejong 1A / TOPIK I) courses sit at the lower end; full TOPIK II preparation packages cost more. Always confirm what is included — books, mock tests, and TOPIK registration are sometimes extra.

What a good course should include

  • A clear path from beginner Hangul to your target TOPIK level
  • Native or highly experienced Korean instructors
  • Speaking, listening, reading and writing practice — not just grammar
  • Regular mock TOPIK tests and feedback
  • Guidance connecting your language study to admission and visa
